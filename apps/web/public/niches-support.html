Help with your workspace.

For connection, publishing, account or privacy requests, contact the Niches operator.

Email Niches support

What to include

Include your workspace email, the platform, the affected post title and the visible error message. A screenshot of the account status can help. Do not send passwords, access tokens, verification codes or private source videos.

Common questions

Why can’t my TikTok account connect?

TikTok access is currently restricted to authorised sandbox testers while approval is pending. A planning entry does not grant TikTok permissions. Check the account on TikTok’s authorisation screen and its eligibility.

Why is my test video private?

Unaudited TikTok Direct Post integrations are restricted to private creator accounts and Only you visibility. Niches displays the audiences TikTok makes available to the creator.

Is an inbox-delivered video already published?

No. Open the notification in the connected account’s TikTok mobile app to edit and decide whether to post it.

How do I disconnect a social account?

Manage the connection in Niches Accounts. You can also revoke Niches through the social provider’s own app/account security settings. Revocation can stop pending deliveries.

How do I request account deletion or a copy of my data?

Email support from your workspace email. State whether you want account deletion, a data copy or another privacy request. We may verify ownership before acting. See the Privacy Policy for details.

What if content infringes my rights?

Send the platform or Niches post reference, identify the work and explain your rights and concern. Do not include unrelated private information. We will investigate under the Terms of Service.

Before sending another copy

Check the calendar’s account result and the target platform first. A processing delay is not proof of failure. Retrying a completed delivery can create a duplicate post.
