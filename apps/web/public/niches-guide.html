Creator guide

Prepare it. Review it. Send it.

Use Niches with original content and accounts you are authorised to manage.

Set up your workspace

  1. Open Niches and sign in or create an account. Use your own email and a unique password.
  2. Create a niche for the subject you work on, then organise its accounts into brands.
  3. Open Accounts and connect a supported social account through that provider. Check the account name on its authorisation screen. Adding an account for planning does not grant publishing access.
  4. Upload original media through the content library. Keep your own copy of source videos; current uploads are limited to 25 MiB per file.

Prepare a post

  1. Open Publish, choose the brand and media, then enter a title and caption.
  2. Check each destination checkbox. Remove any account that should not receive this post.
  3. Save and enter publishing review. Saving alone does not publish the draft.
  4. Inspect the account preview and provider settings. Choose Publish now or an available scheduled time, then give final approval.

Scheduling authorises a later delivery to the selected accounts. Removing a source video or revoking a connection can prevent a queued delivery from completing.

Connect TikTok

  1. In Accounts, choose Add another / reconnect for TikTok.
  2. Check the TikTok creator shown by TikTok. Use Switch account if it is not yours.
  3. Review profile-information, direct-post and draft-upload permissions, the Terms and Privacy Policy. Authorise only if you agree.
  4. Return to Niches and check the Connected label and creator name.

Direct Post

  1. Select Direct Post in the delivery control.
  2. Choose an audience from the creator’s current available options. Select comment, duet and stitch controls where permitted. No interaction is enabled by default.
  3. Declare AI-generated or commercial content when applicable. Review the music confirmation and any relevant branded-content policy.
  4. Confirm the destination and content rights, then approve the delivery.
  5. Open the calendar’s account status. Processing means TikTok is still working. Published means provider confirmation has been received.

Upload to the TikTok inbox

  1. Select Send to TikTok inbox and approve sending the selected video to that creator.
  2. Submit the delivery and wait for Inbox Delivered in the account status.
  3. Open TikTok on the connected creator’s phone. In Inbox / System notifications, find TikTok Platform’s “Your content from Niches is ready” notification.
  4. Open the notification, edit the video, choose the final caption and audience, apply required disclosures and decide whether to post. These final decisions happen inside TikTok.

An inbox upload does not create a public post. TikTok’s desktop notification may direct you to its mobile app to finish editing.

Understand status and recover safely

StatusMeaning and next action
Needs connectionThe planning account is not authorised. Connect the intended owner before sending.
Queued / ProcessingThe delivery is waiting or being processed. Check the existing result before submitting another copy.
Inbox DeliveredTikTok confirmed delivery to its creator inbox. Finish inside TikTok.
PublishedThe platform confirmed publication. Check its own app for visibility and moderation.
Failed / Needs reviewOpen account details for the reason. Correct permissions, media or settings before retrying.
Export reminderA manual-posting reminder, not proof of an API upload.

For a missing notification, verify the TikTok username first and inspect System notifications in the mobile app. Avoid repeated uploads while one is processing.
