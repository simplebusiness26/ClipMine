Know where every post stands

The calendar separates scheduled, processing, published and failed deliveries. Review individual account results when a post has several destinations. An export reminder is shown separately from a provider-confirmed post.

Connections and permissions vary by platform. A planned account must be authorised before it can receive API deliveries. The app does not promise views, reach or platform approval.

Keep control of your content

Workspace media requires authentication. Connecting a social account is a separate authorisation step. You can manage connections in Accounts and revoke Niches in the social platform’s own account settings.
