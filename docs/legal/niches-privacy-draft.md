# Niches Privacy Policy

Updated: 4 November 2026

## 1. Controller and contact

**Niches** is responsible for the personal information described here. Contact for privacy requests and complaints: **Craigs.cartoons98@gmail.com**.

This policy covers the Niches web service at https://niches.ai-seo.workers.dev/. Social platforms also process information under their own policies when you authorise them or publish there.

## 2. Information we use and where it comes from

We receive your email address, password when you register or sign in, session information, workspace/niche details and preferences. Passwords are stored as salted hashes rather than readable passwords.

When you connect a social account, the provider supplies the account identifiers, display/profile information and authorisation tokens permitted by your consent. Niches stores tokens encrypted on the server. It does not receive your social-platform password.

We store media you upload, filenames, file types and sizes, captions, drafts, selected destinations, audience and interaction options, commercial-content disclosures, scheduled times, approval records, provider publication IDs, status and failure information. Media may contain information about other people; only upload material you are authorised to handle.

Service providers may process IP addresses and technical request/security information to deliver and protect the service. Niches also keeps application audit and security records. We do not infer that all information held by an infrastructure provider is copied into the application database.

## 3. Why we use it

We use account, session, workspace and content information to provide the service you request, including private storage, account connection, drafts, approved publishing and scheduling. Where necessary to perform our agreement with you, our basis is contract.

We use proportionate security, fraud prevention, operational diagnosis and support processing for our legitimate interests in running a reliable, secure service. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests; we will assess the request and any applicable grounds to continue.

Where we rely on consent for an optional feature, you can withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing. A platform's OAuth consent controls the permissions it grants; it is not blanket consent for unrelated use of your data. We may also process information to meet legal duties.

## 4. TikTok permissions

user.info.basic allows Niches to identify the TikTok account connected by its owner and display permitted basic profile information.

video.publish is intended for creator-approved Direct Post, including posts Niches sends when an approved schedule becomes due. The creator must select the destination, review the content and available settings, and expressly approve transmission. Provider processing may continue after Niches sends the request.

video.upload permits sending content to TikTok as a draft for you to finish editing and publish within TikTok. This option is not currently available in Niches.

TikTok API use depends on account authorisation and TikTok approval. This policy does not mean the integration is approved or available. We do not use TikTok data for unrelated advertising or sell it.

## 5. Sharing and service providers

Cloudflare provides the Niches Worker, database and private beta media storage. Connected TikTok, Meta and Google services receive the information needed for the accounts and publishing actions you authorise. A media transfer may use a short-lived bearer URL accessible to anyone who obtains that link during its lifetime; do not share those links.

If account-recovery email is enabled, an email-delivery provider processes your address and recovery message to deliver it. Contact us for details of the provider used for your request.

We may disclose information when legally required, to protect rights or security, or to authorised support operators where necessary. We do not sell personal information. Niches does not automatically send private media to an AI service merely because it is uploaded.

## 6. International processing

Our Cloudflare infrastructure may process information outside the UK, including in the United States and countries in its global network. Cloudflare’s published Data Processing Addendum provides for international-transfer safeguards, including the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where applicable and standard contractual clauses with the UK Addendum for restricted transfers. You can read the safeguards at https://www.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-customer-dpa/ or contact us for further information. Connected social platforms also process information internationally under their own privacy policies when you connect an account or approve a publication. We do not promise UK-only storage.

## 7. Retention and deletion

Account/workspace information, drafts and media are kept while needed to provide your active service, until removed through available controls or an account-deletion request, unless a specific legal or security need justifies limited retention. Niches does not currently promise an automatic fixed-period purge of inactive accounts or media.

Session records expire after seven days. OAuth connection tokens are retained for the connection's operation until disconnected, invalidated or deleted. Time-limited provider-media links expire after two hours. Approval, publication and audit information may remain where necessary to display history, reconcile publishing, investigate abuse or meet legal duties. We assess continuing need rather than retaining it for unrelated purposes.

You can delete media through the library and disconnect social accounts in Niches. Contact us for account deletion, an export or controls not offered in the interface. We may need to verify ownership before acting. Removing original media can prevent pending posts from completing. Cloud storage, caches and provider recovery mechanisms may have separate deletion timings; no immediate backup-erasure promise is made.

Deleting from Niches or revoking authorisation does not automatically delete posts already published on TikTok or another platform. Manage those posts on the relevant platform.

## 8. Cookies and local state

Niches uses a session cookie for sign-in and short-lived browser-bound state to secure OAuth. The site may retain local interface preferences. These are used for the requested service; the current app does not include an advertising-cookie programme. Infrastructure providers may use security measures needed to protect the service.

## 9. Your rights and complaints

Depending on the processing and applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your personal information, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may withdraw consent where consent is our basis. Contact the address above to exercise rights or complain; we will respond within applicable legal timescales and explain any lawful limits.

You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office: https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/. You do not have to contact us first to exercise that right.

## 10. Security, age and changes

We use access controls, hashed passwords, server-side encrypted connection tokens and scoped media access. No system is guaranteed risk-free. Keep your login secure and tell us about suspected misuse.

Niches is intended for adults aged 18 or over and is not designed to collect children's information knowingly. Contact us if information was supplied contrary to this requirement.

We will update this notice when practices change and identify its revision date. Material changes will receive appropriate notice.
