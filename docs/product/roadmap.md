# Product Roadmap

Dates are deliberately not fixed until team capacity and processing costs are known. The sequence is ordered by risk.

**Current position:** the local/private upload-to-export proof in Phase 0 is implemented. Discovery interviews, permissioned quality evaluation, cost benchmarking and public-ready controls remain open; Phase 1 is not complete.

## Phase 0 — Validation and proof

Outcome: prove that ClipMine can find and render useful moments.

- Interview a narrow creator segment.
- Collect a permissioned evaluation set.
- Implement local/dev upload-to-transcript path.
- Generate candidate timestamps with reasons.
- Render one vertical captioned clip.
- Measure quality, latency and cost per source minute.

Exit gate: reviewers consistently prefer the system's top candidates over random or transcript-only baselines.

## Phase 1 — Private alpha

Outcome: one creator can complete the entire workflow safely.

- Account and private workspace
- Resumable uploads
- Persisted processing jobs
- Candidate review screen
- Basic transcript, trim, crop and caption editor
- Final render and download
- Deletion, usage limits and support tooling
- Core monitoring and automated tests

Exit gate: invited users can process real projects without developer intervention.

## Phase 2 — MVP beta

Outcome: repeatable product with measurable quality.

- Brand kits and caption templates
- Candidate diversity controls
- Better speaker tracking and multi-speaker layouts
- Feedback-driven scoring calibration
- Billing/allowance controls where legally and operationally appropriate
- Onboarding, lifecycle messages and improved recovery
- Accessibility and broader device coverage

Exit gate: repeat usage and project-to-export conversion justify scaling.

## Phase 3 — Distribution

Outcome: creators can move approved outputs into their channels with less friction.

- YouTube authorised upload
- TikTok draft/direct-post integration subject to approval
- Instagram and Facebook publishing subject to account and app eligibility
- Scheduling, publishing status and retry rules
- Per-platform validation and metadata

Exit gate: integrations pass platform review and production monitoring.

## Phase 4 — Teams and learning

Outcome: ClipMine becomes a content workflow, not only a cutter.

- Team workspaces and approvals
- Content calendar
- Performance ingestion where permitted
- Creator-specific preference learning
- Variant experiments and recommendations
- API and integrations for agencies

## Explicitly deferred

- Voice cloning
- Synthetic presenters
- Unattended mass posting
- Arbitrary public-video downloading
- Claims or scores presented as guaranteed virality
