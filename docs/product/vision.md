# Product Vision

## Vision

Make long-form video reusable without making creators become full-time video editors.

## Problem

A podcast, interview, lesson, webinar or talking-head recording may contain many strong short-form moments. Finding them requires watching the recording, understanding context, choosing clean boundaries, reframing landscape footage, writing captions and exporting several versions. That work is repetitive, slow and often prevents useful material from being published.

Existing automatic clipping can produce fragments that begin without context, end before the payoff, frame the wrong person or optimise for superficial excitement. ClipMine should behave more like a careful first-pass editor.

## Product thesis

The winning system will combine semantic understanding, audiovisual signals and human feedback. It will not merely detect peaks. It will identify moments that can stand alone, explain why they were selected, create editable drafts and learn from the creator's choices.

## Initial customer

The initial beta user publishes conversational or educational long-form video and wants regular short-form output without hiring an editor for every recording. Examples include podcasters, interviewers, educators, coaches, founders and small content teams.

The beta should choose one narrow group after discovery interviews. The system must not pretend that one scoring model works equally well for every genre.

## Value proposition

- Hours of review become minutes of selection.
- One source video produces multiple differentiated drafts.
- Every clip remains editable and under creator control.
- Exports are platform-ready without locking the creator into one network.

## Principles

1. **Find complete ideas, not random fragments.**
2. **Creator control beats blind automation.**
3. **Trust is visible.** Explain selections, progress, costs and deletion.
4. **Quality beats clip count.** Five credible drafts are better than twenty weak ones.
5. **Rights come first.** Process content only when the user has authority.
6. **Platform independence.** Keep the core useful even if a social API changes.

## Non-goals

- Guaranteeing views, reach or virality
- Replacing professional long-form editing
- Downloading and republishing third-party videos
- Engagement manipulation or spam distribution
- Generating deceptive speech or impersonations

## North-star outcome

The percentage of processed projects from which the creator exports or approves at least one clip, supported by measures of edit burden, processing reliability and repeat weekly use.
